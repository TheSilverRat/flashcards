# Welcome — how it works

Each deck is one file in this folder. The line starting with "# " is the deck name.
Every card starts with "Q:" and its answer starts with "A:". Both can span several lines.
Anything above the first Q: (like this paragraph) is ignored, so you can keep notes here.

Q: **Swipe up** (or tap) to flip this card
A: This is the answer side.

Tap **Got it** if you knew it. **Missed it** brings the card back at the end of the session and marks it, so you can go over just the missed cards later.

Q: **Swipe left** to skip a card for now
A: A skipped card isn't marked either way.

**Swipe right** brings back the previous card and undoes your answer.

Q: What is the quadratic formula?
A: $$x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$$

Q: Euler's identity
A: $$e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$$

Links $e$, $i$, $\pi$, $1$ and $0$.

Q: Derivative of $\sin(x)$?
A: $$\frac{d}{dx}\sin(x) = \cos(x)$$

Q: Gaussian integral
A: $$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2}\,dx = \sqrt{\pi}$$

Q: How do I change how a deck is studied?
A: Open the deck and tap the **options** button at the top right.

**Basic** (the default) goes through all the cards whenever you like. **Spaced repetition** brings cards back days apart, with daily limits. You can also set the card order and how many cards a session has.
